# Short, reversible probe on the gripper motor only (motor 1).
# Goal: find out whether the servo holds a position (180 deg unit) or keeps
# spinning (360 deg unit). Both outcomes end at the stop pulse, so nothing runs away.
# Run: tools\armctl.ps1 script examples\probe_gripper.txt --port COM4

V
wait 300
R
wait 500
A0
wait 200

# Move 10 degrees away from neutral, slowly.
M1:100:1500
wait 2200

# Back to neutral.
M1:90:1500
wait 2200

# Report state, but stay enabled and holding.
Q
